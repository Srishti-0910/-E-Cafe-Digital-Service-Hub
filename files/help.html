Frequently Asked Questions

How do I fill a legal form? +

Go to the Legal Form Filling section from the home page, click the form you want, and fill it on the official government website. Download it when done.

How do I book a ticket? +

Visit the Ticket Booking section, choose your travel type (train, bus, flight, or movie), and you'll be redirected to the official booking site.

How do I place a print order? +

Go to Print Orders from the home page. Choose Black & White or Color, enter number of pages, upload your file, and proceed to payment.

How do I create an account? +

Click the hamburger menu (☰) and go to Account. Click Register and fill in your name, email, and password to create your E-Cafe account.

How do I contact support? +

Visit the Contact page from the menu, fill out the form with your query, and our support team will respond to your email shortly.
